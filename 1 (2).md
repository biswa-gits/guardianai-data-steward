# GuardianAI — Day 4 Runbook (Build Phase 4): The Dashboard

**Goal of Day 4:** Make everything visible. Deploy the **Streamlit in Snowflake**
app (5 pages) and add the **Governance Recorder Agent** (audit trail). By end of
day: a demo-ready dashboard that tells the whole story in clicks.

## Part A — Governance Recorder Agent (run first)

| Step | File | What it does |
|------|------|--------------|
| 1 | `sql/15_governance_recorder.sql` | Creates `DQ_GOVERNANCE_LOG` + `LOG_EVENT()` proc, backfills the full audit trail from all prior agent work |

Expected: `DQ_GOVERNANCE_LOG` fills with events across 6 agents — OBSERVER,
DIAGNOSIS, IMPACT, REMEDIATION, GOVERNANCE (approvals), VALIDATION.

> This is your Responsible-AI centerpiece: a single immutable-style log showing
> who did what, when, and who approved it.

## Part B — Deploy the Streamlit app

**File:** `app/guardianai_app.py`

**Deploy in Snowsight (2 minutes):**
1. Snowsight → **Projects → Streamlit → + Streamlit App**.
2. Set: **Database** `GUARDIANAI_DB`, **Schema** `CORE`, **Warehouse** `GUARDIANAI_WH`.
3. Delete the boilerplate, paste the full contents of `guardianai_app.py`, click **Run**.
4. `get_active_session()` connects automatically — no credentials needed.

## The 5 pages (and what each proves)

| Page | Shows | Scoring tie-in |
|------|-------|----------------|
| 📊 **Data Trust Overview** | Exec summary paragraph, overall score tile, per-table bars, before→after chart | Business value (25%) |
| 🔍 **Findings Explorer** | All detected issues, filter by table/severity | Snowflake/detection (20%) |
| 🧠 **AI Investigation Center** | Root cause + business impact per issue (Cortex output) | Agentic AI (35%) |
| 🛠️ **Remediation Center** | Fix plan, confidence, risk, approval status, the actual fix SQL | Agentic AI + Responsible AI |
| 📜 **Governance Log** | Full audit trail, filter by agent, human-actor count | Responsible AI (15%) |

## Expected result after Day 4
- App runs with all 5 pages populated from your tables.
- Page 1 shows your **43 → 97** story with a before/after bar chart.
- Page 5 shows the complete audit trail.

## Demo flow to rehearse (uses the pages in order)
1. **Overview** — "Our data trust score was 43/100, CRITICAL." (read the exec summary)
2. **Findings** — "15 issues, 5 critical, detected by deterministic SQL."
3. **Investigation** — open ORPHAN_ORDER: "AI explains root cause + business impact."
4. **Remediation** — "Agent recommends fixes with confidence + risk. Nothing runs
   without human approval. Here's the exact, auditable SQL."
5. **Overview again** — "After approved remediation: 97/100, all tables LOW risk."
6. **Governance** — "Every action is logged. AI advises, SQL executes, humans approve."

## Design notes
- App uses `get_active_session()` (native SiS) and `@st.cache_data(ttl=60)` so it's
  fast and needs no secrets.
- Every page degrades gracefully — if a table isn't populated yet, it shows a hint
  instead of crashing.
- `IMPACT_LEVEL` and risk badges are color-coded for instant visual reads.
