# GuardianAI — Day 2 Intelligence Runbook (Build Phase 2)

**Goal of Day 2:** Add the reasoning layer. The Cortex/CoCo agents read the
15 issues from Day 1 and turn them into **plain-English root causes** and
**executive business impact**. By end of day: AI explains every issue.

## Pre-check: is Cortex enabled? (30 seconds)
Run this in a worksheet first:
```sql
SELECT SNOWFLAKE.CORTEX.COMPLETE('mistral-large2','Say OK');
```
- ✅ Returns text → use the **AI path** (files 07, 08, 09).
- ❌ Errors / not authorized → use the **fallback** (file 10). Your demo still
  works fully; you just swap in curated reasoning instead of live generation.

> Tip: if `mistral-large2` isn't available, try `llama3.1-8b`,
> `snowflake-arctic`, or `mistral-7b` and update the model name in the files.

## Run order

| Step | File | What it does |
|------|------|--------------|
| 1 | `sql/06_create_analysis_table.sql` | Creates `DQ_ISSUE_ANALYSIS` (stores AI output) |
| 2 | `sql/07_diagnosis_agent.sql` | **Diagnosis Agent** → fills `ROOT_CAUSE` |
| 3 | `sql/08_business_impact_agent.sql` | **Business Impact Agent** → fills `BUSINESS_IMPACT` |
| 4 | `sql/09_executive_summary.sql` | **Exec Summary Agent** → one CDO paragraph for Page 1 |
| — | `sql/10_fallback_no_cortex.sql` | Use INSTEAD of 07+08 only if Cortex is off |

## Expected result after Day 2
`DQ_ISSUE_ANALYSIS` has **15 rows**, each with:
- `ROOT_CAUSE` — 1–2 technical sentences (why it happened)
- `BUSINESS_IMPACT` — 2 executive sentences (why it matters)
- `IMPACT_LEVEL` — HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW (derived from severity, sortable)

Plus `DQ_EXEC_SUMMARY` has one leadership-ready headline paragraph.

Sample (fallback wording for the 5 CRITICALs):
- **DUPLICATE_CUSTOMER_ID** → "Same customer ingested twice from CRM and LEGACY
  sources…" → *"Customers may be counted twice, inflating reports…"*
- **ORPHAN_ORDER** → "Referential integrity not enforced…" → *"Orders pointing to
  non-existent customers break revenue-to-customer reporting and audits."*

## Design principle to say out loud in your demo
> "Detection is deterministic SQL — reliable and auditable. AI is used **only**
> for reasoning, impact, and narration. That separation is what makes GuardianAI
> trustworthy, not just clever." (Directly supports the Responsible AI weight.)
